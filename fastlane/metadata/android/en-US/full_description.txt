A native Android companion app for Hermes Agent, the open source AI assistant by Nous Research.

Control your Hermes agent directly from your phone over your local network, without needing a laptop or another messaging platform.

Features:
• Chat over WebSocket with markdown rendering
• Switch AI models and providers
• Stream and filter system logs
• Manage agent profiles and settings
• Pull to refresh for live updates
• System notifications for incoming messages
• Material You dynamic theming on Android 12+
• Works over your local Wi-Fi, with no internet routing required

All network communication uses cleartext HTTP and WebSocket by default, designed for trusted local networks.
