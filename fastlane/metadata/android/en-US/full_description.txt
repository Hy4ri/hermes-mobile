Hermes Mobile is a native Android app for Hermes Agent, the open source AI agent by Nous Research. It connects to the Hermes dashboard on your computer or server, so you can talk to your agent and check on it from your phone.

What you can do:
• Chat with your agent, send photos, files, and voice notes, and answer approval prompts
• Reply straight from notifications
• Switch models and providers, and manage profiles, skills, plugins, and toolsets
• Create and edit cron jobs
• Stream and filter live logs, check usage analytics, and edit environment keys
• See the status of the gateway, MCP servers, and messaging channels
• Track tasks on Kanban boards and browse past sessions
• Lock the app with your fingerprint or screen lock, or keep a shared phone on one profile
• Pick one of seven themes, or use Material You colors on Android 12+

It works on your home network, and over the internet when the dashboard is behind HTTPS. The app also supports client certificates (mTLS), private certificate authorities, Cloudflare Access, and a built-in SSH tunnel. Chat history and saved credentials are encrypted on the phone.

You need Hermes Agent with its dashboard running (hermes dashboard). Setup guide: https://github.com/Hy4ri/hermes-mobile/wiki/Connecting-to-Hermes
